1. Site Name

Proposed Name: Napio Beauty & Lifestyle Accessories

Reason for Selection: I chose the name Napio because it is personal to me, and I want to use it as my brand name. Adding "Beauty & Lifestyle Accessories" tells visitors what the shop is about.

Optional Domain Availability: napiobeauty.com

2. Site Purpose

The Napio Beauty & Lifestyle Accessories website will show my handmade beauty products and lifestyle accessories and help customers find out what I offer.

  • The Home page will introduce my brand and show featured products.
  • The Product Catalog will list hair accessories, epoxy resin mirrors and keyholders, lip glosses, and personal care items. Each product will show a price and description.
  • The About & Contact page will tell my brand story, explain how to care for products, and give customers a way to ask questions or request custom orders.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

These are questions that students, young adults, and gift shoppers might have when they visit the website:

Scenario 1: "What handmade beauty accessories are available, and how much do they cost?"

Scenario 2: "Can I order a personalized gift set or ask about a product's materials and delivery?"

Scenario 3: "How do I clean and store my handmade resin accessory so it lasts?"

4–5. Color Schema and Document Use

This plan uses Bubblegum Pink, white, soft pink, and near-black. These colors are also used in the design of this document.

Primary Color: Bubblegum Pink

Hex: #FF9EBB

Usage: Header, primary buttons, section heading accents, card borders, and decorative highlights.

Secondary Color: Clean White

Hex: #FFFFFF

Usage: Page cards, main content areas, white text contrast, and clean negative space.

Background Color: Soft Pink White

Hex: #FFF4F7

Usage: The page background and subtle content sections to keep the design soft and readable.

Text Color: Pure Near-Black

Hex: #1A1A1A

Usage: Body copy, headings, labels, navigation text, borders, and footer backgrounds for high contrast.

6. Typography

This site plan uses Montserrat and Open Sans. Both fonts are used in the document.

Montserrat (Headings & Navigation)

Weights: 600 (Semi-Bold), 700 (Bold)

Application: Used for the page title, section headings, navigation links, and button text.

Open Sans (Body Text)

Weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-Bold)

Application: Used for paragraphs, product descriptions, scenarios, and form labels so the text is easy to read.
